# Two-phone test for v237 (voice & video + sideways board)

Phones: Karim's iPhone 16 Pro signed in as **test**, an Android (Pixel 7 class) as **test2**, PIN 1234 for both.
Before you start: both phones on the home-screen app, the corner shows **v237**, voice-config.json has
`"only": ["test", "test2"]` and `"sidewaysOnly": ["test", "test2"]`, rotation lock ON on both phones, hold them upright.
Tick each line on both phones unless it says one. Write down anything odd (what you did, what you saw, which phone).

## A. Join (2 min)
1. Open the app upright. The lobby is upright. Both sit at the same table. The "Talk while you play" card appears once,
   pick **Video and voice** on the iPhone, **Voice only** on the Android.
2. Camera/mic permission prompts appear once. Say "one two" on each: the other phone hears it, the white ring + sound bars
   show on the talker's picture.
3. Start the game. The board turns sideways on both (phone top on your LEFT), corners hug the long edges, nothing under the
   camera cutout, the round menu button is fully visible.

## B. Video (3 min)
4. iPhone: tap your camera badge. Your face shows in your own corner (mirrored) and in the Android's corner for you,
   upright (not on its side, not upside down). If it is turned, note which way.
5. Android: camera on too. Both faces live on both phones. Turn-taking: the gold frame moves to the active player with a
   gentle pulse; the inactive corners are dimmed but their video keeps moving.
6. Play 5 turns with both cameras on. Rolls, card flies, the robber fly all look normal. Note any stutter or heat.

## C. Interruptions (5 min)
7. Android: press the power button, wait 20 s, unlock, return. Expected: its badges show mic OFF and camera OFF in red on
   BOTH phones while it was away; on return the toast says the mic is back, camera stays off until tapped; the iPhone
   never saw a frozen frame labelled "on".
8. iPhone: receive a phone call (have someone call you), decline it after 10 s. Voice comes back within a few seconds,
   board unchanged. Then answer a call, hang up after 15 s, same check.
9. iPhone: connect AirPods during the game. Voices move to the AirPods within ~5 s; game sounds too; your mic still works
   (ask the Android to confirm).
10. Both: switch the iPhone to 4G (Wi-Fi off). Video and voice continue; note the delay before they recover if they drop.

## D. Touch on the turned board (3 min)
11. Roll a 7 (or wait for one): drag the robber to another hex; it follows your finger, the tick confirms, the robber lands
    where you dropped it. Never "robber in the sea".
12. Emoji tray: open it, scroll it up and down by dragging, swipe sideways to the texts, send one, hold an emoji and throw it
    at a player.
13. Pinch to zoom and pan the board with one finger. Double-check the board does not scroll the whole page.
14. Settings → Sideways board → **Flip it over**. The board turns the other way (phone top on your RIGHT); the faces turn with
    it; repeat 11 and 12 once. Flip back.

## E. Long camera run (10 min)
15. Both cameras on for 10 minutes of normal play. At the end note: phone warm or hot, battery % drop on each phone,
    any frame freeze, any "no voice" / "can't hear" tags.

## F. Leave and return
16. Android: swipe to the home screen for 2 minutes, come back. Camera badge honest (red = not sending), the board still
    correct, the call still up. iPhone: same.
17. Leave the game (menu → Leave) and rejoin from the lobby. Voices continue, cameras off until tapped.

Walkie-talkie test

This page isn't the game. It only tests whether this phone can record and hear. Everything it finds goes straight to Claude on the laptop. Laptop: not connected
1Sound check

Ringer switch on, volume up. Tap: you should hear a short beep.

2Hear the laptop (mic not used yet)

The laptop speaks one sentence to you.

3Record, the way v208 did

Tap, allow the microphone, then count out loud "one, two, three, four". It stops by itself after 4 seconds.

4Hear the laptop again (after using the mic)
5Fix try: back to the loudspeaker

Tells the phone we're done with the mic, then the laptop talks again.

6Record, the way voice notes do

Tap and count "one, two, three, four" again.

7Record, third way

Tap and count again.

8Hear the laptop one last time

All done? Tell Claude on the laptop.
